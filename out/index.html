General Course

6-Month Foundation (A2)

A 6-month English course designed to help learners communicate confidently in everyday situations. Students develop the four skills while improving their vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation through interactive lessons.

  • Build vocabulary and grammar skills

Intermediate Fluency (6 Months)

A 6-month course for intermediate learners focused on building fluency and confidence in spoken English. Students improve their speaking, listening, pronunciation, and advanced vocabulary through immersive and interactive practice.
